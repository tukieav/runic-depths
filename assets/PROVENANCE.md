# Asset provenance

- `hero-atlas.png`: original five-character portrait atlas generated for Runic Depths on 2026-09-05 with OpenAI image generation. No reference image, existing game artwork, logo, or named artist was supplied. Used on the optional character-selection panel.
- `models/*.glb`: five original hero characters authored in Blender 4.5 LTS with 32-bone rigs and eight animation clips each. Reproducible authoring script: `scripts/build-character-assets.py`; editable source scenes: `source-art/characters/`. See `models/README.md` and its manifest.
- `enemies/*.glb`, `enemies/lod/*.glb`: thirty original enemy identities, each with a purpose-built articulated rig, equipment, action clips and simplified Performance variant. Shared PBR atlases, authored vertex palettes and editable Blender scenes come from `scripts/build-enemy-assets.py`. See `enemies/README.md`.
- `textures/*.webp`: fifteen original albedo/normal/roughness maps authored deterministically with Python; generator and licensing are documented in `textures/README.md`.
- `props/*.glb`: two original modelled props authored with the offline Three.js GLTFExporter pipeline in `scripts/build-surface-props.mjs`; see `props/README.md`.
- Additional architecture, modelled botanical dressing, articulated fallback creatures, typed projectiles and interface ornament are code in `src/renderer.js`, `src/gothic-architecture.js`, `src/garden-detail.js`, `src/enemy-fallback.js`, `src/projectile-presentation.js` and `src/style.css`.
- No Quaternius, Kenney, commercial marketplace or other third-party art pack is bundled. Those were evaluated as alternative sources; all new model and texture assets were created for this project.
- Campaign, characters and bilingual text are authored for this game in `src/content.js` and `src/i18n.js`.
- The original chapter scores and effects are generated by `scripts/build-audio-assets.py`; `src/audio.js` also retains synthesized fallbacks. No third-party music recordings or samples are included.
- Three.js is distributed under its MIT license; the shipping bundle includes `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- The locally bundled meshoptimizer decoder uses the MIT license reproduced in `MESHOPTIMIZER-LICENSE.txt`.
- CrazyGames SDK v3 is loaded from its official hosted endpoint when applicable; it is not redistributed inside the ZIP.

## Cinematic update 2.2

The character workshop, 32-joint rigs, eight clips per character and four 512px
maps are original project-authored assets. Standard glTF quantization reduces
attribute storage without an external codec. The original 26 Ogg audio files,
including six stereo chapter compositions, a percussion stem and nineteen sound
effects, are generated by `scripts/build-audio-assets.py`; their provenance and
hashes are recorded in `assets/audio/`. No Diablo models, textures, music, names
or recordings are included. Diablo is a visual reference supplied by the user,
not the name or source of this game's assets.
